## What is happening?

CHG0080982 STMS NET - Calgary and Interior Data Centre WAN Routers Replacement - Refresh MEL devices

WAN routers are being replaced. See the [TIB](https://ociomysc.service-now.com/sp?id=kb_article&table=u_kb_template_technical_information_bulletin&sys_id=9ca4dbc53b7f471059d9740f23e45a48&view=ess) for more details.

## When?

Starting at Sunday, Nov 15 at 1:00 AM and finishing before 6:00 AM

## Will there be an impact on the Platform apps?

On Sunday, November 15, there will be two planned failover events during which no service outage is expected. However, if the activity requires a full outage, the Calgary data centre may experience a service interruption of up to 30 minutes, expected to occur between 1:00 AM and 2:00 AM.

If the additional change window is required on Sunday, November 29, there will be a service outage through the Interior data centre of up to 30 minutes, expected to occur between 01:00 A.M and 02:00 A.M. 

## Do I need to do anything?

No action required

*Check the MS Teams Openshift-alerts channel for the announcement of when the change is complete and check the health of your app.*

## Where do I get help if my app doesn't work after the change is complete?

Each Platform application has an assigned DevOps Specialist within the Ministry so contact them first. If you don't know who your assigned DevOps Specialist, check with the app's Product Owner.

The DevOps Specialist will troubleshoot the issue with the app and if they need help, they will reach out to the Platform Services Team and the Developer Community in MS Teams.
